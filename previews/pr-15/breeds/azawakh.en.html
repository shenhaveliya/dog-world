Azawakh

אזוואק

Size: Large

An elegant West African sighthound — slim, fast, aloof with strangers and devoted to its people.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 10–14 years

Shedding: Very low

Character: Noble, sensitive, independent

Suitable for: Experienced owners, warm climate, fenced yards

Origin: Mali / Niger

Weight: 15–25 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 2 hours

Training difficulty: Medium

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ⚠️ With caution

Recommended food: Complete large-breed dry food (AAFCO) — joint support. 26–30% protein, 14–18% fat. glucosamine/chondroitin for joints. 2 measured meals daily — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
