Miniature Schnauzer

שנאוצר ננסי

Size: Small

The small version of the schnauzer with a signature beard. Alert, friendly, and barely sheds.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 12–15 years

Shedding: Low

Character: Alert, friendly, smart

Suitable for: Allergy-prone homes, apartments, families

Origin: Germany

Weight: 5–9 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete small-breed dry food (AAFCO) — large kibble. 24–28% protein, 8–12% fat. Low–moderate fat — pancreatitis risk. 2–3 meals daily — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
