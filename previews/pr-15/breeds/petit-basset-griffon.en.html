Petit Basset Griffon Vendeen

Size: Small

A small shaggy French hound — cheerful, sociable and an avid scent-trailer.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 12–15 years

Shedding: Low

Character: Cheerful, sociable, lively

Suitable for: Families, fenced yard

Origin: France

Weight: 11–18 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1.5 hours

Training difficulty: Medium

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes
