Cairn Terrier

קיירן טרייר

Size: Small

The famous dog from 'The Wizard of Oz' (Toto). Small, brave, independent, with a huge personality.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 12–15 years

Shedding: Medium

Character: Brave, independent, lively

Suitable for: Apartments, families, basic training

Origin: Scotland

Weight: 6–8 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 0.75 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes
