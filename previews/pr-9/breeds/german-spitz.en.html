German Spitz

ספיץ גרמני

Size: Small

A small fluffy European spitz — alert, smart and suited to apartment life.

Energy level: Moderate

Lifespan: 13–15 years

Shedding: High

Character: Alert, loyal, vocal

Suitable for: Families, apartments

Origin: Germany

Weight: 8–13 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete small-breed dry food (AAFCO) — large kibble. 22–26% protein, 12–15% fat. omega-3 (EPA/DHA) for coat health. 2–3 meals daily — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
