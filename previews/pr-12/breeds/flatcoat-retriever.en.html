Flat-Coated Retriever

Size: Large

The 'Peter Pan' of dogs — remains puppy-like for life. Happy, friendly, and full of joy.

Energy level: Very high

Lifespan: 8–12 years

Shedding: Medium

Character: Joyful, friendly, energetic

Suitable for: Active families, hunting, sports

Origin: England

Weight: 27–36 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 2 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes
