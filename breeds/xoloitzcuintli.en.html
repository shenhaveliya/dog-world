Xoloitzcuintli

שולוטסקווינטלי

Size: Medium

An ancient, sacred Mexican breed — mostly hairless. Calm, loyal, and unique.

Energy level: Medium

Lifespan: 13–18 years

Shedding: Low

Character: Calm, loyal, reserved

Suitable for: Allergy-prone homes, warm climates, adults

Origin: Mexico

Weight: 4–25 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1 hours

Training difficulty: Medium

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete medium-breed dry food (AAFCO). 22–26% protein, 10–14% fat. Sensitive skin — quality protein; sun protection. 2 measured meals daily — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
