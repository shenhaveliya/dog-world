Slovak Cuvac

סלובאק צ׳ובאק

Size: Giant

A white Slovak livestock-guardian giant — devoted, calm and steady in defense.

Energy level: Moderate

Lifespan: 11–13 years

Shedding: High

Character: Protective, calm, loyal

Suitable for: Farms, large yards

Origin: Slovakia

Weight: 31–45 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1 hours

Training difficulty: Medium

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete medium-breed dry food (AAFCO). 22–26% protein, 12–15% fat. omega-3 (EPA/DHA) for coat health. 2 measured meals daily — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).
