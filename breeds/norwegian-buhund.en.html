Norwegian Buhund

נורווגי בוהונד

Size: Medium

A light, cheerful Norwegian spitz — smart, lively and sociable, devoted to family.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 12–15 years

Shedding: High

Character: Cheerful, smart, friendly

Suitable for: Active families, yard

Origin: Norway

Weight: 12–18 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1.5 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete medium-breed dry food (AAFCO). 26–30% protein, 14–18% fat. omega-3 (EPA/DHA) for coat health; zinc and omega-3 for skin health. 2 measured meals daily — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
