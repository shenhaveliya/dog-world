Norwegian Elkhound

אלקהאונד נורווגי

Size: Medium

An ancient Norwegian hunting dog that hunted moose. Brave, loyal, and thrives in cold climates.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 12–15 years

Shedding: High

Character: Brave, loyal, friendly

Suitable for: Cold climates, hunting, active families

Origin: Norway

Weight: 20–25 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1.5 hours

Training difficulty: Medium

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete medium-breed dry food (AAFCO). 22–26% protein, 12–15% fat. omega-3 (EPA/DHA) for coat health; glucosamine/chondroitin for joints; zinc and omega-3 for skin health. 2 measured meals daily — amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
