Italian Greyhound

גרייהאונד איטלקי

Size: Small

A tiny, delicate sighthound — a miniature greyhound. Loves cuddles, warmth, and resting on the couch.

Energy level: Medium

Lifespan: 12–15 years

Shedding: Low

Character: Gentle, affectionate, sensitive

Suitable for: Apartments, seniors, warm climates

Origin: Italy

Weight: 3–5 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 0.5 hours

Training difficulty: Medium

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ⚠️ With caution

Recommended food: Complete small-breed dry food (AAFCO) — large kibble. 22–26% protein, 10–14% fat. 3 small meals daily — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
