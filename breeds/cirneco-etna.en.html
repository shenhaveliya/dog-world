Cirneco dell'Etna

צ׳ירנקו דל אטנה

Size: Medium

A small, elegant Sicilian sighthound — energetic but gentle and affectionate.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 12–14 years

Shedding: Very low

Character: Sweet, elegant, energetic

Suitable for: Active families, fenced yard

Origin: Italy (Sicily)

Weight: 8–12 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1.5 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete medium-breed dry food (AAFCO). 26–30% protein, 14–18% fat. 2 measured meals daily — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).
