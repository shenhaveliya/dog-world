Brussels Griffon

גריפון בריסל

Size: Small

A Belgian toy with a remarkably human face full of expression — smart, sensitive and charming.

Energy level: Moderate

Lifespan: 12–15 years

Shedding: Low

Character: Comical, devoted, sensitive

Suitable for: Adults, apartments

Origin: Belgium

Weight: 3–5 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ⚠️ With caution

Recommended food: Complete small-breed dry food (AAFCO) — large kibble. 22–26% protein, 12–15% fat. strict weight management; smaller, easily digestible meals. 3 small meals daily — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
