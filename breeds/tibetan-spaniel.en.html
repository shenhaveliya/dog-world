Tibetan Spaniel

ספניאל טיבטי

Size: Small

A small Tibetan temple dog — sociable, alert and smart, ideal for apartment life.

Energy level: Moderate

Lifespan: 12–15 years

Shedding: Moderate

Character: Sociable, alert, noble

Suitable for: Families, apartments

Origin: Tibet

Weight: 4–7 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Small-breed dry food (large kibble), omega-3 for coat health. 2–3 small meals daily.

General guidance only — adjust type and amount to your dog's age, weight, and health. Consult your vet.
