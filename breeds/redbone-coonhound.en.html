Redbone Coonhound

רדבון קונהאונד

Size: Large

A rich red American hound — friendly, alert and with a deep voice for night hunting.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 11–12 years

Shedding: Moderate

Character: Friendly, loyal, energetic

Suitable for: Hunters, active families

Origin: USA

Weight: 20–32 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 2 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Large-breed formula with joint support, high-protein for active dogs, omega-3 for coat health. 2 meals daily.

General guidance only — adjust type and amount to your dog's age, weight, and health. Consult your vet.
