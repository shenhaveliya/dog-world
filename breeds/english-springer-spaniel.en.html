Size: Medium

A cheerful, active hunting dog with long ears. Eager to please, easy to train, and great with families.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 12–14 years

Shedding: Medium

Character: Cheerful, affectionate, energetic

Suitable for: Active families, hunting, training

Origin: England

Weight: 18–25 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1.5 hours

Training difficulty: Very easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes
