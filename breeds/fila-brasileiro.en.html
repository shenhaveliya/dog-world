Fila Brasileiro

פילה ברזיליירו

Size: undefined

A large Brazilian mastiff — suspicious of strangers, fiercely loyal to its household.

Energy level: Moderate

Lifespan: 9–11 years

Shedding: Moderate

Character: Protective, loyal, wary

Suitable for: Very experienced owners, fenced yard

Origin: Brazil

Weight: 40–80 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1.5 hours

Training difficulty: Medium

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete giant-breed dry food (AAFCO) — controlled growth and joint support. 22–26% protein, 12–15% fat. omega-3 (EPA/DHA) for coat health; glucosamine/chondroitin for joints. 2–3 smaller meals, slow feeder — amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).
