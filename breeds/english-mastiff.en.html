English Mastiff

מסטיף אנגלי

Size: Large

A calm, gentle giant — one of the heaviest breeds in the world. Loyal and protective of the family.

Energy level: Low

Lifespan: 8–10 years

Shedding: Medium

Character: Calm, protective, friendly

Suitable for: Spacious homes, calm families

Origin: England

Weight: 54–100 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 0.5 hours

Training difficulty: Medium

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete giant-breed dry food (AAFCO) — controlled growth and joint support. 24–28% protein, 8–12% fat. glucosamine/chondroitin for joints; strict weight management. 2–3 smaller meals, slow-feed bowl — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
