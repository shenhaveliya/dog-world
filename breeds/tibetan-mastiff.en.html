Tibetan Mastiff

מסטיף טיבטי

Size: Large

A Tibetan giant with a lion-like mane. An ancient guardian — fiercely protective of family and territory.

Energy level: Medium

Lifespan: 10–14 years

Shedding: High

Character: Protective, independent, reserved

Suitable for: Very experienced owners, fenced yards, guarding

Origin: Tibet

Weight: 32–73 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1 hours

Training difficulty: Very hard

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ⚠️ With caution

Recommended food: Complete giant-breed dry food (AAFCO) — controlled growth and joint support. 22–26% protein, 10–14% fat. omega-3 (EPA/DHA) for coat health; glucosamine/chondroitin for joints. 2–3 smaller meals, slow feeder — amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
