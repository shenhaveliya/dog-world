Cesky Terrier

צ׳סקי טרייר

Size: Small

A relatively quiet Czech terrier — friendly with a soft, silky coat that needs trimming.

Energy level: Moderate

Lifespan: 12–15 years

Shedding: Very low

Character: Calm, friendly, loyal

Suitable for: Families, apartments

Origin: Czech Republic

Weight: 6–10 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Small-breed dry food (large kibble). 2–3 small meals daily.

General guidance only — adjust type and amount to your dog's age, weight, and health. Consult your vet.
