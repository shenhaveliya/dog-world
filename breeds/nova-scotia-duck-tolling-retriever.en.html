Nova Scotia Duck Tolling Retriever

ריטריבר נובה סקוטיה

Size: Medium

The smallest retriever — bright red, smart, charming and excels in water games.

Energy level: Very high

Lifespan: 12–14 years

Shedding: Moderate

Character: Smart, energetic, friendly

Suitable for: Active families, water lovers

Origin: Canada (Nova Scotia)

Weight: 17–23 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 2 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Balanced medium-breed formula, high-protein for active dogs, omega-3 for coat health. 2 meals daily.

General guidance only — adjust type and amount to your dog's age, weight, and health. Consult your vet.
