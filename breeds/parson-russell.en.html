Parson Russell Terrier

פארסון ראסל טרייר

Size: Small

A taller working English terrier — very energetic, smart and independent.

Energy level: Very high

Lifespan: 13–15 years

Shedding: Low

Character: Brave, energetic, lively

Suitable for: Very active people, yard

Origin: England

Weight: 6–8 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 2 hours

Training difficulty: Medium

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Small-breed dry food (large kibble), high-protein for active dogs. 2–3 small meals daily.

General guidance only — adjust type and amount to your dog's age, weight, and health. Consult your vet.
