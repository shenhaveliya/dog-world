Dandie Dinmont Terrier

דנדי דינמונט

Size: Small

A unique Scottish terrier with a domed top-knot of hair — brave, charming and even-tempered.

Energy level: Moderate

Lifespan: 12–15 years

Shedding: Very low

Character: Brave, calm, loyal

Suitable for: Families, apartments, seniors

Origin: Scotland

Weight: 8–11 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete small-breed dry food (AAFCO) — large kibble. 22–26% protein, 12–15% fat. 2–3 meals daily — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).
