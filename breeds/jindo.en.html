Jindo

ג׳ינדו קוריאני

Size: Medium

A legendary Korean breed renowned for its loyalty — smart, independent with a strong prey drive.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 12–15 years

Shedding: High

Character: Loyal, brave, independent

Suitable for: Experienced owners, no small pets

Origin: South Korea

Weight: 14–23 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 2 hours

Training difficulty: Medium

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete medium-breed dry food (AAFCO). 26–30% protein, 14–18% fat. omega-3 (EPA/DHA) for coat health. 2 measured meals daily — amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
