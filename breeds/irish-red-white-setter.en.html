Irish Red and White Setter

סטר אירי אדום-לבן

Size: Large

A more balanced cousin of the Irish Setter — red and white, friendly and great with kids.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 11–15 years

Shedding: Moderate

Character: Friendly, energetic, affectionate

Suitable for: Active families, hunters

Origin: Ireland

Weight: 23–32 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 2 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete large-breed dry food (AAFCO) — joint support. 26–30% protein, 14–18% fat. omega-3 (EPA/DHA) for coat health; glucosamine/chondroitin for joints. 2 measured meals daily — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).
