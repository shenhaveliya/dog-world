Havanese

האוונזי

Size: Small

Cuba's national dog. Small, cheerful, very sociable, and ideal for apartment living.

Energy level: Medium

Lifespan: 13–16 years

Shedding: Low

Character: Cheerful, social, affectionate

Suitable for: Apartments, seniors, families with kids

Origin: Cuba

Weight: 3–6 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 0.5 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete small-breed dry food (AAFCO) — large kibble. 22–26% protein, 10–14% fat. single-protein or limited-ingredient for sensitivities. 2–3 meals daily — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
