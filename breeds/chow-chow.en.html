Chow Chow

צ׳או צ׳או

Size: Medium

An ancient Chinese breed with a lion-like mane and blue tongue. Independent, proud, and reserved with strangers.

Energy level: Low

Lifespan: 9–13 years

Shedding: High

Character: Independent, dignified, aloof

Suitable for: Experienced owners, adult households, early socialization

Origin: China

Weight: 20–32 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 0.5 hours

Training difficulty: Hard

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ⚠️ With caution

Recommended food: Complete medium-breed dry food (AAFCO). 24–28% protein, 8–12% fat. omega-3 (EPA/DHA) for coat health; glucosamine/chondroitin for joints; strict weight management; smaller, easily digestible meals. 2 measured meals daily — amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
