Affenpinscher

אפנפינשר

Size: Small

A bold German toy breed with monkey-like face — fearless, mischievous and endlessly entertaining.

Energy level: Moderate

Lifespan: 12–14 years

Shedding: Low

Character: Fearless, comical, curious

Suitable for: Adults, apartments, people with humor

Origin: Germany

Weight: 3–6 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1 hours

Training difficulty: Medium

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ⚠️ With caution

Recommended food: Complete small-breed dry food (AAFCO) — large kibble. 22–26% protein, 12–15% fat. smaller, easily digestible meals. 3 small meals daily — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
