Collie

Size: Large

The famous "Lassie" — a gentle, intelligent long-coated Scottish herder devoted to its family.

Energy level: Moderate

Lifespan: 12–14 years

Shedding: High

Character: Gentle, smart, protective

Suitable for: Families, kids, first-time owners

Origin: Scotland

Weight: 23–34 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1.5 hours

Training difficulty: Very easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes
