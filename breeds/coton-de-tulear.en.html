Coton de Tulear

קוטון דה טוליאר

Size: Small

A fluffy white toy from Madagascar — joyful, friendly, hypoallergenic and infectiously happy.

Energy level: Moderate

Lifespan: 14–17 years

Shedding: Very low

Character: Happy, sociable, affectionate

Suitable for: Families, apartments, allergy-prone homes

Origin: Madagascar

Weight: 4–7 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1 hours

Training difficulty: Very easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Small-breed dry food (large kibble). 2–3 small meals daily.

General guidance only — adjust type and amount to your dog's age, weight, and health. Consult your vet.
