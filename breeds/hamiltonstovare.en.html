Size: Large

A tri-colored Swedish hunting hound — independent afield, sweet and sociable at home.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 11–14 years

Shedding: Moderate

Character: Friendly, energetic, devoted

Suitable for: Hunters, active families

Origin: Sweden

Weight: 23–27 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 2 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes
