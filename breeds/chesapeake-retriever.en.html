Chesapeake Bay Retriever

ריטריבר מפרץ צ׳ספיק

Size: Large

A tough American working retriever. Excels at retrieving from cold water — loyal and independent.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 10–13 years

Shedding: Medium

Character: Loyal, tough, independent

Suitable for: Hunting, waterside, active owners

Origin: USA

Weight: 25–36 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1.5 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete large-breed dry food (AAFCO) — joint support. 26–30% protein, 14–18% fat. glucosamine/chondroitin for joints. 2–3 smaller meals, slow feeder — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).
