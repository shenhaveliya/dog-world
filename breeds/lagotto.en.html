Lagotto Romagnolo

לגוטו רומניולו

Size: Medium

A curly Italian water dog famed as a truffle hunter — smart, sweet and minimal-shedding.

Energy level: Moderate

Lifespan: 14–17 years

Shedding: Very low

Character: Smart, sweet, active

Suitable for: Families, allergy-sensitive homes

Origin: Italy

Weight: 11–16 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1.5 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete medium-breed dry food (AAFCO). 22–26% protein, 12–15% fat. single-protein or limited-ingredient for sensitivities. 2 measured meals daily — amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).
