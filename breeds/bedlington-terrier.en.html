Bedlington Terrier

בדלינגטון טרייר

Size: Small

The lamb-like terrier — woolly, hypoallergenic, surprisingly fast and tough.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 12–16 years

Shedding: Very low

Character: Sweet, swift, brave

Suitable for: Allergy-sensitive homes, families, apartments

Origin: England

Weight: 8–10 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1.5 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete small-breed dry food (AAFCO) — large kibble. 26–30% protein, 14–18% fat. Low-copper diet — veterinary guidance required. 2–3 meals daily — amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
