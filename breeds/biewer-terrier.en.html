Biewer Terrier

ביוור טרייר

Size: Small

A tri-color, hypoallergenic cousin of the Yorkie — joyful, sensitive with silky long hair.

Energy level: Moderate

Lifespan: 12–15 years

Shedding: Very low

Character: Joyful, affectionate, alert

Suitable for: Apartments, adults, allergy-sensitive homes

Origin: Germany

Weight: 2–4 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 0.5 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ⚠️ With caution

Recommended food: Small-breed dry food (large kibble). 2–3 small meals daily.

General guidance only — adjust type and amount to your dog's age, weight, and health. Consult your vet.
