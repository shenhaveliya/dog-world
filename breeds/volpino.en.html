Volpino Italiano

וולפינו איטליאנו

Size: Small

A small white Italian spitz — alert, smart and a loyal companion who loves to chat.

Energy level: Moderate

Lifespan: 13–15 years

Shedding: Moderate

Character: Alert, loyal, vocal

Suitable for: Families, apartments

Origin: Italy

Weight: 4–6 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Small-breed dry food (large kibble), omega-3 for coat health. 2–3 small meals daily.
