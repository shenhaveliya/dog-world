Drever

דרבר

Size: Medium

A short-legged Swedish hound — excellent at trailing, friendly and cheerful with family.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 12–14 years

Shedding: Moderate

Character: Friendly, alert, energetic

Suitable for: Hunters, active families

Origin: Sweden

Weight: 14–16 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1.5 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Balanced medium-breed formula, high-protein for active dogs, omega-3 for coat health. 2 meals daily.
