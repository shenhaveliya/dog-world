Sussex Spaniel

ספניאל סאסקס

Size: Medium

A heavy-built golden liver English spaniel — calm, easygoing, rare but charming.

Energy level: Low

Lifespan: 12–15 years

Shedding: Moderate

Character: Calm, friendly, devoted

Suitable for: Families, quiet households

Origin: England

Weight: 16–20 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete medium-breed dry food (AAFCO). 24–28% protein, 8–12% fat. omega-3 (EPA/DHA) for coat health; strict weight management. 2 measured meals daily — amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
