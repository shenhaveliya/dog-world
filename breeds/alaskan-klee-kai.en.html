Alaskan Klee Kai

קלי קאי אלסקי

Size: Small

A miniature, playful version of the Husky — smart, alert with strangers, and very active.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 12–16 years

Shedding: High

Character: Smart, energetic, loyal

Suitable for: Active people, no toddlers

Origin: USA (Alaska)

Weight: 4–10 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1.5 hours

Training difficulty: Medium

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ⚠️ With caution

Recommended food: Complete small-breed dry food (AAFCO) — large kibble. 26–30% protein, 14–18% fat. omega-3 (EPA/DHA) for coat health; zinc and omega-3 for skin health. 2–3 meals daily — amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
