Shar Pei

שאר פיי

Size: Medium

An ancient Chinese dog with characteristic wrinkles. Independent, loyal, and protective.

Energy level: Medium

Lifespan: 8–12 years

Shedding: Medium

Character: Independent, calm, loyal

Suitable for: Experienced owners, quiet families

Origin: China

Weight: 18–30 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1 hours

Training difficulty: Hard

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete medium-breed dry food (AAFCO). 22–26% protein, 10–14% fat. Quality protein; gradual changes — skin and GI sensitivity. 2 measured meals daily — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
