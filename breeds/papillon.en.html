Papillon

פפיון

Size: Small

A tiny French dog with butterfly-shaped ears. Remarkably smart, lively, and one of the easiest to train.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 13–16 years

Shedding: Medium

Character: Smart, lively, friendly

Suitable for: Apartments, training, light dog sports

Origin: France

Weight: 3–5 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 0.5 hours

Training difficulty: Very easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete small-breed dry food (AAFCO) — large kibble. 22–26% protein, 12–15% fat. strict weight management. 3 small meals daily — amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
