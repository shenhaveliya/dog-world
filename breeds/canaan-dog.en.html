Canaan Dog

כלב כנעני

Size: Medium

Israel's native desert pariah dog — intelligent, alert and a phenomenal survivor.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 12–15 years

Shedding: Moderate

Character: Alert, loyal, independent

Suitable for: Active people, yard

Origin: Israel

Weight: 16–25 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1.5 hours

Training difficulty: Medium

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes
