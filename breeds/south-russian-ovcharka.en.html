South Russian Ovcharka

אובצ׳ארקה דרום-רוסי

Size: Giant

A shaggy white Russian livestock-guardian giant — determined, independent, fiercely protective.

Energy level: Moderate

Lifespan: 9–11 years

Shedding: High

Character: Protective, independent, resolute

Suitable for: Farms, very experienced owners

Origin: Russia / Ukraine

Weight: 48–80 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1.5 hours

Training difficulty: Medium

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete medium-breed dry food (AAFCO). 22–26% protein, 12–15% fat. omega-3 (EPA/DHA) for coat health. 2 measured meals daily — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
