Artois Hound

האונד מארטואה

Size: Medium

An ancient French hound, a hunter's companion — steady, persistent and with a superb nose.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 10–14 years

Shedding: Moderate

Character: Steady, devoted, even-tempered

Suitable for: Hunters, farms

Origin: France

Weight: 28–30 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 2 hours

Training difficulty: Medium

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete medium-breed dry food (AAFCO). 26–30% protein, 14–18% fat. omega-3 (EPA/DHA) for coat health. 2 measured meals daily — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).
