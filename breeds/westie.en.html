West Highland White Terrier

ווסטי טרייר

Size: Small

A small, white Scottish terrier with a giant personality. Cheerful, friendly, and low-maintenance — great for apartments.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 13–15 years

Shedding: Low

Character: Cheerful, friendly, lively

Suitable for: Apartments, families, kids

Origin: Scotland

Weight: 7–10 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete small-breed dry food (AAFCO) — large kibble. 22–26% protein, 12–15% fat. probiotics/gradual diet transitions. 2–3 meals daily — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).
