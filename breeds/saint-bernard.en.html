Saint Bernard

סנט ברנארד

Size: Large

A gentle giant from the Swiss Alps. Calm, patient, and great with children.

Energy level: Medium

Lifespan: 8–10 years

Shedding: High

Character: Gentle, patient, loyal

Suitable for: Families with kids, large homes, cool climate

Origin: Switzerland

Weight: 65–120 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1 hours

Training difficulty: Medium

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete giant-breed dry food (AAFCO) — controlled growth and joint support. 24–28% protein, 8–12% fat. omega-3 (EPA/DHA) for coat health; glucosamine/chondroitin for joints; strict weight management. 2–3 smaller meals, slow feeder — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).
