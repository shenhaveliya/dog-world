Border Collie

Size: Medium

One of the smartest dogs in the world. Loves to learn and needs challenges.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 10–14 years

Shedding: Medium to high

Character: Smart, energetic, disciplined

Suitable for: Advanced training, sports, active families

Origin: Scotland / England

Weight: 14–22 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 2 hours

Training difficulty: Very easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ✅ Yes
