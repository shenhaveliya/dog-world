Hanoverian Scenthound

האונד הנובר

Size: Large

A German tracking hound, unique for finding wounded game — calm and reliable.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 10–14 years

Shedding: Moderate

Character: Calm, focused, loyal

Suitable for: Professional hunters

Origin: Germany

Weight: 25–40 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 2 hours

Training difficulty: Medium

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Large-breed formula with joint support, high-protein for active dogs, omega-3 for coat health. 2 meals daily.
