Japanese Chin

צ׳ין יפני

Size: Small

A small Japanese aristocratic toy — cat-like in nature, loves laps and easy to care for.

Energy level: Low

Lifespan: 10–14 years

Shedding: Moderate

Character: Noble, calm, affectionate

Suitable for: Adults, apartments

Origin: Japan

Weight: 2–5 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 0.5 hours

Training difficulty: Easy

Good with cats: ✅ Yes

Good with kids: ⚠️ With caution

Recommended food: Small-breed dry food (large kibble), lower fat for weight control, omega-3 for coat health. 2–3 small meals daily.

General guidance only — adjust type and amount to your dog's age, weight, and health. Consult your vet.
