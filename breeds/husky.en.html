Siberian Husky

האסקי סיבירי

Size: Medium

A beautiful, very energetic dog. Less suited for those without time for walks.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 10–14 years

Shedding: Medium to high

Character: Independent, energetic, friendly

Suitable for: Active owners, hikes, sports

Origin: Siberia / Russia

Weight: 16–27 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 2 hours

Training difficulty: Hard

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete medium-breed dry food (AAFCO). 26–30% protein, 14–18% fat. omega-3 (EPA/DHA) for coat health. Working dog — calories match activity; zinc for skin health. 2 measured meals daily — amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
