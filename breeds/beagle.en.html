Beagle

ביגל

Size: Medium

A friendly, curious hunting dog that loves family. Has an excellent sense of smell.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 12–15 years

Shedding: Medium

Character: Curious, friendly, lively

Suitable for: Families, kids, fenced yards

Origin: England

Weight: 9–14 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1.5 hours

Training difficulty: Hard

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete medium-breed dry food (AAFCO). 22–26% protein, 12–15% fat. strict weight management. Food-motivated — strict portions, limit treats. 2 measured meals daily — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
