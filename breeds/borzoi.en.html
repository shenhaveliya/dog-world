Borzoi

בורזוי

Size: Large

A noble, swift Russian sighthound. Calm at home but extremely fast in open spaces.

Energy level: Medium

Lifespan: 9–14 years

Shedding: Medium

Character: Noble, calm, independent

Suitable for: Spacious homes, fenced yards

Origin: Russia

Weight: 27–48 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 1 hours

Training difficulty: Hard

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete giant-breed dry food (AAFCO) — controlled growth and joint support. 22–26% protein, 10–14% fat. glucosamine/chondroitin for joints. 2–3 smaller meals, slow-feed bowl — Amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).
