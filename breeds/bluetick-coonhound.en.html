Bluetick Coonhound

בלוטיק קונהאונד

Size: Large

An American hunter with a striking "ticked" blue coat, ringing bay and a sweet-tempered nature.

Energy level: High

Lifespan: 11–12 years

Shedding: Moderate

Character: Friendly, smart, energetic

Suitable for: Hunters, active families, yard

Origin: USA

Weight: 20–36 kg

Recommended daily exercise: 2 hours

Training difficulty: Medium

Good with cats: ⚠️ Less recommended

Good with kids: ✅ Yes

Recommended food: Complete large-breed dry food (AAFCO) — joint support. 26–30% protein, 14–18% fat. omega-3 (EPA/DHA) for coat health; glucosamine/chondroitin for joints. 2 measured meals daily — amount by weight, age, and activity (vet).

Based on general nutrition guidelines (AAFCO) and breed traits — not a substitute for veterinary advice. Adjust amounts for age, weight, and health.
